Telecom & Network Division

Telecom & Network Division

PGI Telecom is preparing a dedicated division for the technical and contractual management of value-added telephone services. This is different from the existing Audiotel Premium Pro retail offer, and it is not yet an operational telecom network.

Future business unit. Not yet commercially available.

One company, separate activities

Audiotel Premium Pro currently addresses customers seeking a premium-rate telephone number. PGI Telecom Distribution is the planned network and distribution business, with distinct contractual permissions, technical integrations and accounting analysis.

From numbering to call evidence

Numbering rights, call routing, carrier call-detail records and monitoring must all originate from authorised and verifiable sources. Planned features cannot substitute for real interconnection agreements.

Financial control

Publisher settlements must distinguish estimated call value from confirmed amounts, actual collection and authorised payouts. An incomplete statement or a disputed transaction must be handled as an exception.

Working with international partners

This English-language information supports discussions with non-French partners. It does not announce telecom licences, numbering resources, availability or cross-border services in any additional country.

Questions

Is the operator division already live?

No. Carrier agreements, numbering rights, production tests and financial approvals are still prerequisites.

Can existing Audiotel clients keep their account?

Account continuity is part of the design. Any actual number transfer will depend on the applicable mandates, contracts and technical eligibility.
