Telecom & Network Division

Technical and operational solutions

This guide describes the scope of the proposed telecom distribution platform and the evidence required before each function can become active.

Future business unit. Not yet commercially available.

Numbering resource lifecycle

Resource provenance, assignment, validity periods, suspension and portability must be recorded without rewriting historical ownership. Availability is not promised until formally confirmed.

Carrier interfaces and call routing

Interconnection profiles, routing destinations, change approvals and rollback procedures must be tested using actual carrier data before calls are accepted.

Call-detail records and reconciliation

Call references, dates, beneficiaries and tariffs require cross-checking against carrier statements, recorded disputes and reliable evidence of collection.

Publisher financial reporting

Business Live estimates remain separate from confirmed settlements. Payments to third parties stay disabled until legal, contractual and payment-provider conditions are fulfilled.

Security and service continuity

Role-based access, operational logs, anomaly alerts and escalation procedures are required. A missing data feed must never be represented as verified zero traffic.

Questions

Can telecom partners already connect to the platform?

No live connection is advertised. Interface compatibility must be confirmed against a signed and tested operator specification.

Are publisher payouts automatic today?

No. Preparation and checks are separate from real, authorised money movement.
