Questions

What is PGI Telecom Distribution?

It is a future division intended to manage the operational, contractual and financial workflows of telecom and value-added telephone services.

How does it differ from Audiotel Premium Pro?

Audiotel Premium Pro is the existing customer-facing platform. The Distribution division is a separate proposed infrastructure and operator-facing activity.

Can I obtain an SVA number immediately?

No. The division cannot offer an allocated resource until entitlement, carrier agreements and activation requirements are verified.

Are live call figures the same as money received?

No. Estimated traffic and earnings must be distinguished from reconciled statements, bank receipts and legally payable balances.

Do you provide international numbers?

No international availability is announced. The multilingual website is informational and does not mean PGI holds authorisations or numbering resources abroad.

Will existing customers need a new login?

The design aims to retain the existing PGI account where permitted. Migration and number portability depend on contract and regulatory checks.
